# Day 10 – Submarine Cables & Cybersecurity

## Objective
To understand how global internet traffic physically travels and why submarine cables
are critical to cybersecurity.

## What Are Submarine Cables?
- Fiber-optic cables laid on the ocean floor
- Connect continents and countries
- Carry over 95% of global internet traffic

## Why They Matter in Cybersecurity
- Physical damage can disrupt entire regions
- Cable landing stations are high-value targets
- Cyber warfare includes infrastructure disruption
- Digital systems rely on physical connectivity

## Key Learning
Cybersecurity is not limited to software or networks.
It also includes protecting physical infrastructure.

## Takeaway
The internet is physical.
Security must protect what exists beneath the surface.
