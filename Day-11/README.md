# Day 11 – Submarine Cable Vulnerabilities

## Objective
To understand how physical vulnerabilities in submarine cables impact global cybersecurity.

## Key Vulnerabilities
- Accidental cable cuts (anchors, fishing equipment)
- Geopolitical tensions and international conflicts
- Intentional sabotage and cyber warfare strategies

## Impact of Disruptions
- Internet outages across regions or countries
- Banking and financial service failures
- Communication breakdowns
- Delays or failures in digital investigations

## Key Learning
Cybersecurity extends beyond software and networks.
Physical infrastructure is a critical dependency.
