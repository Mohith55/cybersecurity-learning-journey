# Day 27 – Digital Forensics Basics

## Objective
To understand the fundamentals of digital forensics
and its role in cybersecurity investigations.

## What is Digital Forensics?
Digital forensics involves the identification, preservation,
analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.

## Key Areas
- Disk and file system forensics
- Memory (RAM) forensics
- Log and timeline analysis
- Artifact examination

## Core Principles
- Evidence integrity
- Chain of custody
- Repeatable and verifiable analysis

## Key Learning
Cyber attacks leave digital traces.
Forensics turns those traces into actionable evidence.
